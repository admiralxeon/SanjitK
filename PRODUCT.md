## Platform

## Users

Hiring managers and recruiters filling AI/LLM engineering roles. They arrive from a résumé, LinkedIn or a shared link, usually skimming between other candidates, and need to decide quickly whether Sanjit is worth an interview.

Success is a direct contact: an email or an interview request.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Sanjit Kulkarni, a software engineer moving from 4+ years of real-time 3D and games into AI/LLM engineering. It exists to make that move credible: show the AI work, show the engineering depth behind it, and make reaching him effortless.

## Positioning

Embodied AI: real-time AI that people talk to face to face, such as avatars, kiosks and Holoboxes that ship in physical spaces. The combination of production graphics experience (Unity, Unreal, MetaHuman, AR/VR) and LLM/agent engineering is the claim most AI engineers cannot honestly make.

Agent work (tool calling, pipelines) and the games background support that claim; they are not the headline.

## Product Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Every claim should point at a demo, case study, repo or number. When there is no proof, say less.
2. **Lead with embodied AI.** The face-to-face, real-time AI work goes first; everything else supports that story.
3. **Respect a skimmer's time.** A hiring manager should grasp role, specialty and how to make contact within seconds, on any device.
4. **Private by default for client work.** Show what the work does, never whose it is in the site's own words, and never the code.
5. **Contact is always one step away.** Email, LinkedIn and the résumé are reachable from every page without playing with any interactive piece first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Everything interactive must have a plain, keyboard-reachable path to the same information. Motion must be pausable, and reduced-motion preferences must be honoured. Text meets WCAG AA contrast.
